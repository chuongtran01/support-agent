# Caring for Your Gear

Good care helps your gear last, and keeps it within the warranty. Damage from improper storage is not covered.

## Store it dry

Dry everything completely before putting it away. Damp gear can grow mildew.

## Store it loose

Avoid keeping sleeping bags and insulated jackets compressed for long periods.

## Clean it gently

Follow the care label. Harsh detergents can damage waterproof coatings.
