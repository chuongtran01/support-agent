# Gift Cards

## Buying a gift card

Northwind gift cards are sold in amounts from $25 to $500 and delivered by email within one hour.

## Using a gift card

Enter the gift card code at checkout. A card can be combined with another payment method. Any remaining balance stays on the card.

## Refunds and returns

Gift cards are not refundable and cannot be returned. Lost cards cannot be replaced unless you have the original purchase email.

## Expiration

Northwind gift cards do not expire.
