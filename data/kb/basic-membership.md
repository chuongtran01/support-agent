# Basic Membership

Basic is the free account level. Everyone who creates an account starts on Basic.

## What Basic includes

An account to track orders and returns, and to contact support.

## What Basic does not include

Plus benefits: free standard shipping on every order, 60-day returns and early access to sales.

## Upgrading

You can upgrade to Plus at any time from "Membership" in your account.
