# How to Start a Return

## Before you begin

Have your order number ready. You can find it in your confirmation email or under "Orders" in your account. Check the return window and condition requirements in the Returns Policy.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open "Orders".
2. Choose the order, then select "Start a return".
3. Pick the items you are sending back and tell us why.
4. Print the label if one applies to your return, or follow the on-screen instructions.
5. Pack the items in their original packaging and attach the label.

## After you ship

Your return status appears on the order page. We will email you when we receive the package and again when the refund is sent.

## Need help?

Open a support ticket with your order number and we will walk you through it.
