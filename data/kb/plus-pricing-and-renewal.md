# Plus Pricing and Renewal

## Price

Northwind Plus costs $49 per year.

## Renewal

Plus renews every year on the date you signed up. You will receive an email before it renews.

## Payment

Your saved payment method is charged on the renewal date. If the payment fails, update your payment method in your account.
