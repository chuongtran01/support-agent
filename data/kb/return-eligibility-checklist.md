# Return Eligibility Checklist

Use this list to check whether an item can be returned for a change of mind.

- Within the return window counted from the delivery date
- Unused, in original packaging, with proof of purchase
- Not marked "Final Sale"
- Not a gift card
- Not an opened consumable such as fuel, water treatment or food

If your item fails these checks but is defective, it may still be covered by the warranty. See Warranty vs Returns.
