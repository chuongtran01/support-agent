# When You Are Charged

Your card is charged when your order ships, not when you place it.

## What you see at checkout

At checkout we may place a temporary authorization on your card to confirm it is valid. This is not a charge.

## Orders with several packages

If an order ships in more than one package, you are charged when the order ships.
