# Boot Fit Guide

## Measuring

Measure your foot in the afternoon, when it is slightly larger. Wear the socks you will hike in.

## Checking the fit

You should have about a thumb's width of space in front of your toes when you stand. Your heel should stay put when you walk uphill.

## Not sure?

Try the boots on indoors and exchange for another size within the return window if needed.
