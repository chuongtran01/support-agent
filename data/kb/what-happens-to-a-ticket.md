# What Happens to Your Support Ticket

## Review

A team member reads your ticket during support hours and replies by email.

## When a ticket is needed

Some situations are handled by a team member rather than automatically: conflicting information, missing information, and requests that need manual review.

## Following up

Reply to the email to add details. You can also check the ticket status in your account.
