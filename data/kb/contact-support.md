# Contacting Support

## Chat and tickets

Use chat for quick questions and tickets for anything that needs a team member to look into your order.

## Hours

Chat and tickets are answered Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. Pacific.

## What to include

Include your order number and a short description of the problem. Photos help for damaged or defective items.
