# Shipping Rates and Delivery Times

## Rates

| Method | Cost | Delivery time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | $7.95 | 5 to 7 business days |
| Expedited | $19.95 | 2 to 3 business days |
| Overnight | $34.95 | Next business day, order by 1 p.m. Pacific |

## Free shipping

Standard shipping is free on orders of $75 or more. Plus members get free standard shipping on every order.

## Processing time

Orders ship within 1 business day of being placed. Delivery times above start when the order ships.

## Where we ship

We ship to addresses in the continental United States only. We cannot ship to PO boxes with expedited or overnight service.
