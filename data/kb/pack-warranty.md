# Pack Warranty

Backpacks and packs are covered against manufacturing defects for 3 years from the delivery date.

## What this means

If a strap, buckle or seam fails because of a manufacturing defect within 3 years, open a ticket. We will repair, replace or refund the pack.

## What is still excluded

Normal wear, misuse, modifications and damage from improper storage are not covered, the same as for other products.
