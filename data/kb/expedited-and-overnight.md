# Expedited and Overnight Shipping

## Options

| Method | Cost | Delivery time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Expedited | $19.95 | 2 to 3 business days |
| Overnight | $34.95 | Next business day |

## Overnight cut-off

Order by 1 p.m. Pacific for overnight service. Orders placed later ship the next business day.

## Restrictions

These methods are not available to PO boxes. Delivery times start when the order ships, after the 1 business day processing time.
