# Photos for a Warranty Claim

## What to send

Open a support ticket with your order number and clear photos of the problem.

## Good photos

- Show the whole item, then close-ups of the damage
- Use daylight and a plain background
- Include any tags or labels showing the product name or size

## Review time

We review claims within 2 business days.
