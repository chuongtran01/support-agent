# Returning Packs and Bags

## Return empty and clean

Please empty and clean backpacks and bags before returning them. A pack that has been loaded and carried is considered used and does not qualify for a change-of-mind return.

## Trying a pack at home

You can adjust the straps and load a pack lightly indoors to check the fit. Avoid carrying it outside.

## Pack warranty

Backpacks and packs have a longer warranty than most gear. See the Pack Warranty article.
