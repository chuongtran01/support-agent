# Receipts and Invoices

You can download a receipt for any order from the order page in your account.

## Proof of purchase for returns

Your order number in your account counts as proof of purchase. You do not need to keep the paper receipt.

## Tax

Sales tax, where it applies, is calculated at checkout from your shipping address and shown on your receipt.
