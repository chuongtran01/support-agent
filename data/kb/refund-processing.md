# How Refunds Are Processed

## Refund method

Refunds are sent to the original payment method. We cannot refund to a different card or give cash.

## Timing

Allow 5 to 7 business days after we receive your return. Your bank may need a few extra days to post the credit.

## Partial refunds

If you return only some items in an order, you are refunded for those items only. The refund cannot be more than the price you paid for the item.

## Shipping charges

Original shipping charges are refunded only when the return is for a defect or an error on our side.

## Large refunds

Refunds above $200 are reviewed by a member of our team before they are approved, so they can take an extra business day or two.

## Multiple refunds

If you have requested several refunds in a short period, a team member may review your latest request before it is processed.
