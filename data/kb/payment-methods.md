# Accepted Payment Methods

We accept credit and debit cards. You can also pay all or part of an order with a Northwind gift card.

## Saving a card

You can save a card in your account for faster checkout. Saved cards can be removed at any time.

## Not accepted

We do not accept cash, checks or money orders.
