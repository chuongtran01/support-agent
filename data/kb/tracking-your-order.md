# Tracking Your Order

## Where to find tracking

When your order ships we send an email with a tracking link. You can also open "Orders" in your account and select the order.

## Multiple packages

If your order ships in several packages, each one has its own tracking link.

## Tracking not updating

Tracking can lag by a few hours after a package changes hands. If it does not update for several days, open a support ticket with your order number.
