# Northwind Plus Benefits

Plus members get:

- Free standard shipping on every order
- 60-day returns, counted from delivery
- Early access to sales

## Price

Plus costs $49 per year.

## What Plus does not change

Final Sale items stay final for Plus members. The Trial Pitch window for tents is 14 days for everyone.
