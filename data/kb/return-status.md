# Checking the Status of a Return

## Where to look

Open "Orders" in your account and select the order. The return status is shown beside each item.

## Status meanings

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Label created | You started the return; we are waiting for the package |
| Received | The package arrived and is being inspected |
| Refunded | We sent the refund to your original payment method |

## Timing

After we receive and inspect the return, refunds take 5 to 7 business days to appear. Your bank may take extra time to post them.
