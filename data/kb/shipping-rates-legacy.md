# Shipping Rates

## Rates

| Method | Cost | Delivery time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | $5.95 | 5 to 7 business days |
| Expedited | $15.95 | 2 to 3 business days |

## Free shipping

Standard shipping is free on orders of $50 or more.
