# Cancelling Plus

## How to cancel

Open "Membership" in your account and choose "Cancel Plus". You can cancel any time.

## When it ends

Your benefits continue until the end of the year you have paid for. After that your account becomes a Basic membership.

## Refunds

We do not refund part of a paid year when you cancel.
