# Final Sale Items

## What is final sale?

Some clearance items are marked "Final Sale" on the product page and in your cart. The marking is shown before you pay.

## Policy

Final sale items cannot be returned or refunded, and they cannot be exchanged. This applies to every customer, including Plus members.

## Defects

Final sale does not affect your warranty. If a final sale item arrives defective, open a ticket and we will repair or replace it under the warranty terms.

## Order cancellation

You can cancel an order containing final sale items at no cost before it ships.
