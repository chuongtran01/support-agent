# What Happens If a Payment Fails

## One retry

If your card is declined at shipment, we try again once after 24 hours.

## If it fails again

The order is cancelled and nothing is charged. You can place the order again with a different payment method.

## Avoiding failures

Check that your card is valid and has enough available credit. Update your payment method in your account if it has changed.
