# Return Shipping Labels and Fees

## When return shipping is free

Returns are free when the item is defective or when we made a mistake, such as sending the wrong item.

## When a label fee applies

For any other return, a $7.95 label fee is deducted from your refund.

## Using your own label

You may use your own carrier and label instead. We cannot reimburse the cost of your own label.

## Packing tips

Use the original box if you still have it. Remove old shipping labels or cover them so the carrier scans the new one. Keep your drop-off receipt until the refund arrives.
