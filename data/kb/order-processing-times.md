# Order Processing Times

Orders ship within 1 business day of being placed. Delivery times begin when the order ships, not when you place it.

## Orders placed on weekends

Orders placed on a weekend or holiday are processed on the next business day.

## Large orders

Large orders may ship in more than one package. You receive tracking information for each one.
