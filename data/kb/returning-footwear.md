# Returning Footwear

## Trying on boots and shoes

Try footwear on indoors, on a clean, soft surface such as carpet. Please do not wear it outside.

## What we cannot accept

Footwear with visible outdoor wear, including scuffed soles, mud or dirt, cannot be returned for a change of mind.

## Size exchanges

If the fit is not right, you can exchange for another size within the return window. Start the exchange from your order page.

## Defects

If a pair of boots fails because of a manufacturing defect, it is covered by the warranty. Open a ticket with photos.
