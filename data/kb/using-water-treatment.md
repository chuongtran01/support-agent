# Using Water Treatment Products

## Follow the label

Each product has its own contact time and dosage. Read the label before your trip.

## Opened packages

For safety and hygiene reasons, opened water treatment products cannot be returned.

## Storage

Keep tablets and drops in their original container, away from heat and sunlight.
