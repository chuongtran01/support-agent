# My Order Is Late

## Check processing first

Orders ship within 1 business day of being placed. Delivery times count from the day the order ships.

## Check tracking

Open your tracking link for the latest status. Weather and carrier volume can cause delays.

## Still no update

Open a support ticket with your order number. We will contact the carrier on your behalf.
