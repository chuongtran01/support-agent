# Warranty Repair, Replacement or Refund

When a claim is approved, Northwind chooses whether to repair, replace or refund the item.

## Repair

We may repair the item and send it back to you.

## Replacement

If we cannot repair it, we send a new one of the same item where available.

## Refund

If neither is possible, we refund the amount you paid to your original payment method.
