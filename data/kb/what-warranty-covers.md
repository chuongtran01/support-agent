# What the Warranty Covers

## Coverage

Northwind products are covered against manufacturing defects for 1 year from the delivery date.

## Examples of defects

- A zipper that fails under normal use
- A seam that comes apart
- A tent pole that arrives cracked

## Not covered

Normal wear and tear, misuse, modifications and damage from improper storage are not covered.
