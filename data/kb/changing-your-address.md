# Changing Your Shipping Address

## Before your order ships

Open a support ticket right away with your order number and the corrected address. We can update it if the order has not shipped.

## After it ships

Once an order has shipped, the address cannot be changed. If the package cannot be delivered, it will be returned to us and we will contact you.

## Tip

Check your address carefully at checkout, including apartment numbers.
