# Warranty Overview

## Coverage

Northwind products are covered against manufacturing defects for 1 year from the delivery date. Backpacks and packs are covered for 3 years.

## What is not covered

Normal wear and tear, misuse, modifications, and damage from improper storage are not covered.

## Making a claim

Open a support ticket with your order number and clear photos of the problem. We review claims within 2 business days.

## Remedy

We choose whether to repair, replace or refund the item. Warranty claims are not limited by the 30-day return window or by final sale status.
