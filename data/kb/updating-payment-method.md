# Updating Your Payment Method

Open "Payment methods" in your account. Add a new card and choose it as your default. You can remove old cards at any time.

## Plus renewals

Plus renews using your default payment method. Update it before the renewal date to avoid a failed payment.

## Orders already placed

A change to your default does not change the card for an order that is waiting to ship. Open a support ticket if you need to change the payment method on a pending order.
