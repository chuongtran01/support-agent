# Tent Care Basics

## After a trip

Set the tent up at home and let it dry completely before packing it. A wet tent stored in its sack can mildew.

## Cleaning

Use lukewarm water and a soft sponge. Avoid detergents and washing machines.

## Storage

Store the tent loosely in a cool, dry place. Keep poles and stakes with it so nothing gets lost.
