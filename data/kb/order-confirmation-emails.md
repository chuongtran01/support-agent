# Order Confirmation Emails

## What you receive

After you place an order we email you a confirmation with your order number and the items you bought. We send another email when it ships, with tracking.

## Did not get it?

Check your spam folder. You can also find every order in your account under "Orders".

## Wrong email address

Open a support ticket if you entered the wrong email address and we can help update it.
