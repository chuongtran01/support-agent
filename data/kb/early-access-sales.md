# Early Access to Sales

Plus members can shop sales before they open to everyone.

## How you find out

We email Plus members when a sale is about to start. You can also check the "Sales" page while signed in.

## Final Sale items

Some clearance items in a sale are marked "Final Sale". These cannot be returned or refunded, and Plus does not change that.
