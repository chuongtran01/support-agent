# Returning Apparel

## Condition

Apparel must be unworn and unwashed, with all tags still attached.

## Base layers and socks

Base layers and socks can be returned only when they are sealed in their original packaging. Once opened, they cannot be returned for a change of mind.

## Fit exchanges

You can exchange apparel for a different size or color within the return window. See the Exchanges article.

## Defects

If a seam or zipper fails because of a manufacturing defect, open a ticket under the warranty.
