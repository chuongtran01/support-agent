# Plus Returns: 60 Days

Plus members can return eligible items within 60 days of the delivery date. Everyone else has 30 days.

## Same rules otherwise

Items still need to be unused, in original packaging and with proof of purchase. Final Sale items, gift cards and opened consumables cannot be returned.

## Tents

The Trial Pitch window for tents is 14 days from delivery for all customers, including Plus members.
