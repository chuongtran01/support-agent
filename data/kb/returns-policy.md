# Returns Policy

We want you to be happy with your gear. If something is not right, this page explains how returns work at Northwind Outfitters.

## How to start a return

Sign in, open your order, and choose "Start a return". Select the items, tell us why, and print the prepaid label if one applies. If you need help, open a support ticket with your order number.

## Return window

You can return most items within 30 days of the delivery date. The clock starts on the day the carrier marks your package as delivered, not the day you placed the order.

Plus members have 60 days from delivery. See the membership articles for details on Plus.

## Condition requirements

Items must be unused, in their original packaging, with proof of purchase. Tags and protective films should still be attached. We cannot accept items that smell of smoke, pets or heavy storage.

## Return shipping

Returns for defects or our own mistakes ship free. For any other return, a $7.95 label fee is deducted from your refund. You can also drop the package at a carrier location with your own label, but we cannot reimburse that cost.

## Refund timing and method

Refunds go back to the original payment method. After we receive and inspect your return, allow 5 to 7 business days for the refund to appear. Your bank may take additional time to post it.

## Exchanges

You can exchange an item for the same item in a different size or color within the return window. If the new size costs more, we charge the difference. If it costs less, we refund the difference.

## Special categories

### Footwear

Footwear may be tried on indoors on a clean surface. Shoes and boots with visible outdoor wear cannot be returned for a change of mind.

### Apparel

Apparel must be unworn and unwashed with all tags attached. Base layers and socks are returnable only when sealed in their original packaging.

### Packs and bags

Backpacks should be returned empty and clean. A pack that has been loaded and carried is considered used.

### Tents and shelters

Tents and shelters are inspected carefully on return. Any sign of use, dirt, stakes marks or odors means the item no longer qualifies as unused under the general rules above.

There is one exception. Under our Trial Pitch program, a tent or shelter may be returned after a single use, within 14 days of delivery, if it is undamaged, clean and dry, and all parts and the original stuff sack are included. Trial Pitch returns are limited to one per customer in any 12 months, and the shorter 14-day window applies even to Plus members.

### Opened consumables

Fuel canisters, water treatment products and food cannot be returned once opened, for safety and hygiene reasons.

## Items that cannot be returned

- Items marked "Final Sale" at purchase
- Gift cards
- Opened consumables

See the final sale and gift card articles for more on each.

## Damaged or defective items

If your item arrives damaged or fails because of a manufacturing defect, it is covered by our warranty regardless of the return window. Open a ticket with photos and your order number and we will repair, replace or refund it.

## Contact

Chat and tickets are answered Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. Pacific.
