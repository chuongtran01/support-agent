# My Package Says Delivered but I Can't Find It

## First steps

Check around your door, with neighbors and in any shared mailroom. Packages are sometimes left in a safe place nearby.

## Still missing

Open a support ticket with your order number and delivery address. A team member will look into it.

## Prevent it next time

Ship to an address where someone can receive packages, or to a PO box with standard shipping.
